# CORE Admission Boundary v1.0 — Ingestion Gate Contract (Minimal)

Authority:
- SEE Constitution v1.0
- SEE Constitution Addendum A (A1–A15)

Status: LOCKED DERIVATION
Scope: Boundary + admission semantics only. No execution logic. No governance logic.

---

## 1. System Boundary Start

1.1 SEE SHALL begin exclusively at CORE ingestion of a Structured Pack.
[Derived From: SEE Constitution Addendum A → Article A1]

1.2 No other event, artifact, or process SHALL constitute SEE activation.
[Derived From: SEE Constitution Addendum A → Article A1]

---

## 2. Admissible Object

2.1 The only admissible object for entry into SEE SHALL be the Structured Pack.
[Derived From: SEE Constitution Addendum A → Article A2]

2.2 Any object not admitted as a Structured Pack SHALL NOT be treated as inside SEE.
[Derived From: SEE Constitution Addendum A → Article A2]

---

## 3. Validity Creation at Ingestion

3.1 Validity SHALL be created exclusively at CORE ingestion.
[Derived From: SEE Constitution Addendum A → Article A2]

3.2 No external component SHALL define admissibility or validity prior to ingestion.
[Derived From: SEE Constitution Addendum A → Article A3]

---

## 4. Rejection Ontology (Non-Admission)

4.1 Rejection SHALL NOT be a failure of execution.
[Derived From: SEE Constitution Addendum A → Article A13]

4.2 Rejection SHALL produce no execution trace or state within SEE.
[Derived From: SEE Constitution Addendum A → Article A13]

4.3 Rejection SHALL produce no SEE artifact.
[Derived From: SEE Constitution Addendum A → Article A13]

4.4 Rejection SHALL NOT represent negative knowledge.
[Derived From: SEE Constitution Addendum A → Article A13]

4.5 Rejection SHALL represent only non-admission into SEE.
[Derived From: SEE Constitution Addendum A → Article A13]

4.6 A boundary event record MAY exist outside SEE indicating that admission did not occur; such a record SHALL NOT be a SEE artifact and SHALL NOT be part of SEE state.
[Derived From: SEE Constitution Addendum A → Article A13 (patched)]

---

## 5. Admission Authority & Prohibitions

5.1 Admission authority SHALL be defined only by constitutional admission authority and prohibitions.
[Derived From: SEE Constitution Addendum A → Article A3]

5.2 No component outside CORE ingestion SHALL:
- validate admissibility
- create validity
- produce SEE-ingestable objects
[Derived From: SEE Constitution Addendum A → Article A3]
